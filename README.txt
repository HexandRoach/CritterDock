CritterDock Cozy Care - prototype

Open project.godot in Godot 4 standard edition and press F5.
This is a new complete project. Keep your old project as a backup.
All GDScript indentation uses tabs.

Three clicks hatch the egg. Click Pip to pet him.
Meals opens three recipes. Cooking takes 5-9 seconds; Pip then walks to eat.
Fresh food spoils after ten active minutes. Clean removes spoiled food.
Sleep sends Pip to the bed; Rest wakes him. Follow and Play are explicit modes.
Click a fresh bowl to eat. Drag the ball to play.
Clean restores hygiene and washes an empty/dirty bowl.

Needs decline over tens of active minutes, not seconds.
Health declines only during sustained empty hunger or energy.
Cozy mode defaults ON and prevents death. Switching Cozy on does not revive a dead pet.
Standard permits death from sustained neglect. Health warnings appear above the room.
No offline decay: closing the application pauses time.
Saves every 15 seconds and on normal close/Esc using Godot user:// storage.
This project uses a new save filename and does not import earlier prototype saves.
After death a restart button creates a new egg and replaces that save.

Visuals are stylized procedural vector shapes, not realistic rendered art.
The care simulation is simplified: no inventory, shopping, dietary balance,
illness diagnoses, personality generation, bath animation, or desktop overlay yet.
Hygiene/fun/social currently affect presentation or meters, not illness/death.
Food recipes and fullness values are fictional game mechanics, not nutrition advice.

Source generated by the assistant; not executed or parser-tested in Godot.
